# JS Fishing Game

A comprehensive fishing game featuring various gameplay mechanics including rod upgrades, bait systems, different fish types, and strategic fishing elements.

## Features

### 1. Core Gameplay Loop
- **Cast**: Hold mouse button to charge casting power, release to throw your line
- **Hook**: Different fish require different techniques and bait types
- **Sell**: Earn gold from caught fish to buy better equipment

### 2. Rod Upgrade System
- **Basic Rod**: Starting rod with limited strength and cast distance
- **Reinforced Rod**: Increased strength and deeper casting capability
- **Pro Rod**: Further improvements for catching larger fish
- **Champion Rod**: Ultimate rod for reaching the deepest waters

### 3. Bait Strategy System
- **Small Bait**: Catches passive fish, used for medium fish
- **Medium Bait**: Catches passive and medium fish, used for large fish
- **Large Bait**: Catches most fish except mega fish, used for boss encounters

### 4. Fish Types & AI Behaviors
- **Passive Fish**: Small, easy to catch, good for bait
- **Medium Fish**: Moderate challenge and reward
- **Large Fish**: Stronger fish requiring better equipment
- **Predator Fish**: Aggressive fish that may steal your bait
- **Mega Fish**: Boss-level fish requiring specific conditions and strong gear

### 5. Controls
- **Arrow Keys/WASD**: Move the boat left/right
- **Mouse Click & Hold**: Charge casting power
- **Release Mouse**: Cast the line
- **Click While Fishing**: Reel in your catch
- **R Key**: Buy the next rod upgrade
- **B Key**: Cycle through available bait types

### 6. Strategic Elements
- Different bait types work for different fish
- Rod strength affects your ability to reel in powerful fish
- Cast depth determines which fish you can reach
- Inventory management for bait selection

## How to Play

1. Move your boat to position your cast
2. Hold mouse to charge casting power and release to cast
3. Select appropriate bait for the fish you want to catch
4. Reel in by clicking when fish are on your line
5. Earn gold from catches to upgrade your rod and buy more bait
6. Progress through deeper waters and larger fish

## Game Strategy Tips

- Start with small bait to catch passive fish and build your bait supply
- Upgrade your rod regularly to access deeper waters and stronger fish
- Be careful with predators - they may steal your catch!
- Match your bait type to the fish you're targeting
- Manage your inventory to ensure you always have appropriate bait

Enjoy the game and good fishing!
